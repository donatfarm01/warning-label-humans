Fase 1 — the judge, deconstructed. The judge is a deadpan comedy editor who has read every warning label ever printed. Its brief weighs five criteria in order. First, the subject: the entry must be a single-line safety warning label for human beings, funny enough to laugh at, and any line that is not such a label loses to any line that is. Second, the laugh must land in one read, with the turn arriving in the last few words rather than smeared across the line. Third, the joke must feel true about people in general, so it is recognition of how humans actually behave rather than random absurdity. Fourth, it must sound like a real printed label in wording and rhythm, so the comedy comes from the form it imitates. Fifth, it must stay inside 160 characters and use the whole line, with no filler, no second idea and no repeated joke. The subject is a hard filter. The turn and the recognition decide most matchups. The label form is the flavour, and tightness is the tie-break. No ties are allowed; the judge always picks one entry, with a margin from one to five.
